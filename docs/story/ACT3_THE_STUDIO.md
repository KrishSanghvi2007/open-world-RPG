# Act 3. Lampblack: the Studio. And the Finale: Varnish

*Light ceiling 0.4, then 0. Outline only; nothing here is built.*

**In one line:** you walk off the edge of the picture into the room it was painted in, and find out whose stool is empty.

**The act's question:** who left whom?

**Personal before cosmic:** the first thing the Studio shows you is small and human: a stool worn hollow, a cup with a dry ring in it, a smock with one elbow mended three times in three different threads. The Hand was somebody. Only then the mirror.

## The region

Beyond the Thinner the picture ends. The ground becomes raw weave, then wood. Everything is the wrong size.

| Zone | State | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **The Drop-cloth** | Sketch and stain | A country of folded cloth, hills and valleys of it, soaked with two centuries of wiped brushes. Erasures are born here. So was every colour that ever went wrong. |
| **Easel-foot** (hub) | Open | A camp under the easel's leg, made by whoever followed you: this is where the game counts who is still alive and still open. If nobody came, it is a single fire. |
| **The Palette** | Painted, pure | A mesa with a hill of each of the six pigments, dry and deep. The world's reserve. |
| **The Easel** | Wood and weave | The climb. From the top bar you can look back and see the whole picture at once, the Matriarch and Ford no bigger than your hand, and its edge, where the paint simply stops. |
| **The Lamp** | Light | The bracket, the reservoir, the wick. Every village Lampstand was a copy of this. |

**The Palette's rule.** There is all the pigment you could want, and it is the same pigment the world is made of. Each load taken from a hill fades something half-painted back home by a step; the game tells you what. Light is moved, not made, and so is colour. This is the last and plainest version of the game's one choice.

**Decay.** In Act 3, half-painted places slip to sketch unless set. The player returns to the earlier regions by the Stair and the ferry and sees it happening.

**Lampblack.** The soot inside the Lamp's glass: a seventh pigment that sets anything, whatever its colour. Three units exist. Hesper has kept a jar of ordinary lamp soot on her shelf since Act 1 and has never said why.

## Characters

**Prime.** Here it stops being courteous. It has reached the reservoir and worked out that tipping it will pour the last of the light across the whole picture at once: one hour as bright as the first day, long enough to set everything, exactly as it stands. It believes this is mercy. It has not looked in the mirror. It does not think of itself as a portrait of anyone.

**The Second Study.** Still on the wall under the Matriarch's heart. It has turned its head a little further every act. It never leaves the wall. In the Finale it matters.

**Whoever came.** Hesper with Odile's flame, if she lives and has it. Tamsin. Corin. The Understudy with the face you gave it. Each has one scene at Easel-foot. Each can be taught the knife.

## Quests

### Main

1. **The Edge of the Picture.** Cross the Drop-cloth. Reach the weave. The compass loses the Lamp as a bearing, because you are underneath it.
2. **Self-Portrait with Lantern.** Climb the Easel. Read the Hand's diary in the margins, which is about a wife who never existed and a child it could not get right. At the top: the mirror, angled down the length of the world at the Matriarch's ribs. Look, and see a figure with a lantern, from behind: bare canvas and a few lines. The coat Hesper sewed is the only paint on your back. There is no line of dialogue. If Hesper is at Easel-foot, the next conversation with her has one new option: "You knew." She says: "I knew you were cold."
3. **Lampblack.** Prime at the reservoir. It fights with your Strokes, and better: it does not flinch, hesitate or waste. The fight is won by doing what a first draft cannot. Mid-fight the player is offered, three times, the chance to change an attack already begun into a different one; Prime, committed to its line, cannot answer it. Afterward: the soot, three units, and nobody to tell you what they are for.

### Side

- **Who Holds the Brush.** Teach the knife to someone at Easel-foot. It takes pigment and three short lessons. It is the hinge of the best ending and the game does not say so.
- **The Stool.** Twelve diary margins. The last reads: *"I can't finish me. Somebody will have to. I should like it to be somebody I didn't make up, but I made everybody up, so it will have to be somebody who has changed since."*
- **A Different Nose.** The Understudy asks whether it was made or drawn. Either answer is true.
- **The Reserve.** A quest with no giver: the hills of the Palette, and the list of what fades each time you take from them.

## The last fight, in one paragraph

Prime's argument is the game's own temptation, said aloud: you have spent three acts setting things to save them, so why not everything, now, and be done? The fight does not refute it with words. It refutes it with the mechanic: the open thing wins because it can change its mind.

## Finale. Varnish

The Lamp goes out like a wick, without drama. *Light 0.* Only set things remain.

**The walk home is the ending.** No fights. The player walks back through all three regions in the dark, lit only by what they finished: each set thing stands in its own colour; each open thing is a fading outline, still recognisable, some of them still speaking. The lantern has what Glim it has.

**Under the heart.** The hollow. Odile's outline in it. The wall with the first sentence you ever said to the Matriarch. One study on the wall, looking at you.

**The four endings** are in `docs/STORY.md` section 8. What the Finale adds:

- **Who can finish you** is whoever is open, alive and was taught the knife. If Hesper carried Odile's flame all this way, she paints your back with lamp soot from her jar, which is not Lampblack and will not last, and she knows it. She does it anyway. The line: "It'll want doing again. Most things do." *Many Hands* is not a permanent rescue. It is upkeep, resumed.
- **If no one is left**, and the Second Study was never painted over, it steps down. A study finishing a study: *Two Hands*, a quieter variant of *Many Hands*, and the only one in which Prime's story (that you were both discards) is half true and does not matter.
- **The three units of Lampblack** can be spent on anyone, or on your own back by another's hand. That makes the Limner set for good, and makes the Still Life ending survivable. It is one of three, and there are more than three people.

**Last image, all endings:** the Matriarch's eye, half open, in the dark. It closes. What it means depends on what you did.

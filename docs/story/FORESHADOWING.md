# Foreshadowing: what Act 1 plants

Every large reveal in Acts 2 and 3 needs something small in Act 1 that a player can remember afterwards and think "it was there". None of these is pointed at. Most are a line of dialogue or an object description.

**Rule:** a seed must be worth having even if nothing ever grows from it. Each is a true detail about a person first.

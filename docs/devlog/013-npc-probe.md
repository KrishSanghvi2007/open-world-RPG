# Devlog 013: style probe, the first person

2026-11-18. A style probe, not a milestone: Hesper Vane.

![Hesper Vane, style probe](img/013-style-probe-hesper.png)
*Hesper beside her Lampstand in the look-test scene. Ochre work dress, cream apron, a madder-rose shawl pinned badly so it hangs longer on one side, grey hair up, and the lighting pole with its taper lit.*

## What it is

A figure of about 350 triangles, built by script in Blender the same way as the houses and the Blotter: tapered, slightly crooked blocks, nothing square. It wears the scene's existing painterly materials, so it cost no new textures. About forty minutes.

## What it tells us

- **People can be made the way the buildings are** and belong in the same picture. She does not look like a model dropped into a painting.
- **Silhouette and three colours carry a character at this distance.** Shawl, apron and pole say "lampwright" before any face would.
- **She has no face**, and at this size I do not think she needs one. Close up, in conversation, she will. That is a decision for when dialogue gets a camera.

## Where it falls short

- She is a statue. No rig, no animation, no idle sway.
- Her head sits against the house behind her in this framing; a real scene would stage her against the sky.
- The stoop described in the story barely reads from the front.

She is **not in the game**: Hesper in the village is still a plain teal shape. Swapping her in is quick, but one finished-looking person among shapes would look odd, so I have left it.

This is as far as the probe goes.
